# Rock-Paper-Scissors (but the computer doesn't care what you pick)

> A simple, silly, and random version of the classic game.  
> The computer flips a coin instead of playing fair. Yeehaw!

##  What is this?

This here is a **fake** Rock-Paper-Scissors game.  
You type "rock", "paper", or "scissors" — and the computer just randomly says **"You win!"** or **"You lose!"**  

Your choice doesn't matter one bit.  
It's like playing against a chicken that just pecks at two buttons: WIN or LOSE. 

##  How to play

1. Run the program
2. Type: `rock`, `paper`, or `scissors`
3. The computer flips a magical coin
4. You get a random result (win or lose)
5. Play again or walk away like a boss

##  Why would anyone make this?

- For learning Python (super simple code)
- For fun (or frustration)
- To understand `random.choice()`, `while True`, and `if/else`
- To prove that computers can fake anything
